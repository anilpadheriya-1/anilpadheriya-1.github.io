Privacy Policy — Hatchling Merge

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Hatchling Merge ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it on Android. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

1. What data we collect

The Game itself stores your progress (levels, coins, hatched pets, settings) only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

The services enabled depend on the build you install. Ordinary builds use these Google services:

  • Google AdMob (ads) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which asks for consent in regions where the law requires it.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous gameplay statistics). It is switched on only after your consent choice is known.

Voodoo testing builds: Ads and Firebase collection are disabled. These builds instead offer an optional measurement choice before starting GameAnalytics, Meta/Facebook App Events, and Adjust. Selecting No thanks lets you play without starting these services. Selecting Allow enables gameplay analytics and install attribution for testing with Voodoo. No Facebook login is required.

GameAnalytics receives session information and level starts, wins, failures, quits and scores. Meta and Adjust measure installs and app activity to help identify which advertising campaign brought a player to the game. These SDKs may also receive device identifiers, device and operating system information, IP addresses and associated approximate location. We and Voodoo can view the test results through the connected services. Consent choices are stored separately from game progress on your device.

These services may collect:

  • Device or other identifiers: your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), the app instance ID, the app set ID and similar identifiers.
  • App interactions: for example, levels started and finished, pets hatched, in-game coins earned and spent, ads you see or tap, and when the Game is opened.
  • Diagnostics: errors, performance data and technical information about your device (model, operating system version, language, network type).
  • Approximate location: service providers may infer an approximate region (country or city) from your IP address. The Game never asks for, or uses, your precise location.

If you share a hatch clip with the Share button, the clip is created on your device and handed to the app you pick. We do not receive it.

2. How we use data

This data is used to:

  • show ads in the Game, including rewarded ads that you choose to watch;
  • measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud;
  • understand how players use the Game (for example, which levels are too hard), so we can improve it;
  • find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • remember your consent choices where the law requires consent.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to running and improving the Game.

4. Data retention

Progress saved on your device stays there until you use "Reset progress" in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the Game. We do not keep a copy.

Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Firebase project, at most 14 months.

Data already sent to GameAnalytics, Meta or Adjust is retained under the applicable service settings and policies. Turning off measurement or resetting your local progress does not automatically delete data previously sent to these services. Contact us to request help with deletion.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, contact us at the address below and we will help get it removed.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. California residents may also opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.

You can exercise these rights by:

  • Changing your consent: in a Voodoo testing build, open Settings → Privacy choices and select No thanks to disable further publisher measurement. This control is available regardless of region. In ordinary builds, the ad-consent control is shown where required by the consent service.
  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Deleting local game progress: use Settings → Reset progress in the Game. This does not change your separate publisher consent choice. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Hatchling Merge → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device).
  • Requesting deletion of collected data: email cultivatordevloper@gmail.com with the subject Hatchling Merge data deletion. Tell us which app version and platform you used. We will help route the request to the relevant services and may need an app/device identifier to locate records because the Game has no user accounts. Do not send passwords or account secrets. You can also use the privacy contacts and rights-request options in the service policies linked above. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not itself erase previously submitted server records.
