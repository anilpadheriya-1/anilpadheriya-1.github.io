Doodle Merge: Notebook Wars

Grab your pencil. Your notebook is about to go to war.

A free Android game by Cultivator Developer

Free to play No purchases Works offline Ages 13+

Doodle Merge: Notebook Wars is a fast merge-runner played entirely on ruled paper. Steer a squad of scribbled stick figures down the page, grab multiplier gates to grow your crowd, and watch them fuse into bigger, stronger doodles as you swerve.

Screenshots

How it plays

Swerve to merge

Your troops merge as you steer. Swing left and right and pairs fuse together — two scribbles become a capped doodle, two of those become a pencil knight, and so on up to the neon haloed champion. The more you weave, the higher you climb.

Pick your lane

Every gate row is a real decision. Take the x3 and grow the crowd, or gamble on the mystery gate. Steer wrong and a red trap gate wipes out half your squad — or all of it.

100 paper pages

Fight through 100 hand-drawn notebook levels, with a giant boss doodle waiting every tenth page. The early pages are yours for the taking. The last ones will take everything you have.

Fill your sketchbook

Five doodle warriors to discover, from the humble Scribble to the Neon Halo. Merge deep enough to unlock every page.

No paywall, no purchases

There is nothing to buy in this game — not one thing. Every upgrade, every page and every doodle is earned by playing. The game never asks you for money and holds no payment details of any kind. It is funded by advertising alone.

Spend Ink on permanent upgrades: start bigger, merge faster, shoot quicker. Earn Gems by watching a video, trade them for Ink, and build a squad that finally cracks that page you keep losing.

Details

  • Procedurally drawn art — not one image file in the entire game
  • One-thumb controls, made for phones
  • Short runs, quick restarts
  • Plays offline; ads need a connection
  • Android package com.doodlemerge.notebookwars
